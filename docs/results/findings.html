1. Most fitted weights say whose answers a model's answers are near, not which procedures it runs. In 12 of 16 fits the slip rate ε sits at its cap (0.5), and in 14 of 16 the data simulated from the fitted mixture miss at least one of three simple statistics of the real answers. On C E2 colouring, for example, the fitted mixtures expect 0.33 [0.29, 0.38] of Qwen3-32B's answers (thinking off) and 0.39 [0.35, 0.43] of gpt-oss-20b's (low effort) to equal a member's answer; the data have 0.00 and 0.28.

    Interval: 5–95% over 100 fake data sets drawn from each fit. Chance level: none; this checks the fitted model, it is not a test against chance. (b) fitted, but the model does not reproduce the data

  2. gpt-oss at low effort solves most C E2 knapsack items, and when it errs it often gives a greedy rule's answer. These are the only two fits that reproduce their data. gpt-oss-20b gives the optimal packing on 158 of 300 items and gpt-oss-120b on 212 of 300. The weight on EXACT is 0.616 [0.535, 0.688] and 0.739 [0.653, 0.823], against permutation chance levels of 0.039 and 0.051. Where both the model and RATIO-SKIP are wrong, the model gives RATIO-SKIP's answer on 29 of 112 items (20b) and 35 of 79 (120b), against chance levels of 0.008 and 0.009; p = 0.0005 for each, the smallest possible with 2,000 permutations.

    The fitted models reproduce the data: they expect 0.72 [0.69, 0.75] and 0.87 [0.85, 0.90] of answers to equal a member's answer, and the data have 0.72 and 0.87, with ε = 0.29 [0.23, 0.34] and 0.13 [0.08, 0.18]. That makes the weights a fair description of these answers. It does not show that the model runs these procedures. (a) fitted, and the fitted model reproduces the data

    No interval: these are counts, one sample per item. No chance level: this unit has no permutation baseline, because no two items in a cell share their vertex labels, so the within-cell shuffle cannot move any answer. (c) descriptive count

    Interval: none for the counts; 5–95% over 100 fake data sets for the check. Chance level: the within-cell permutation baseline. (c) descriptive count for the agreements; the fits themselves are (b).
